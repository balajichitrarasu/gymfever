CHEST + TRICEPS
Push Day~75 min6 Exercises
Warm-Up Protocol
  • 5 min light treadmill / jump rope
  • 2 × 15 push-ups (slow eccentric)
  • Band pull-aparts 2 × 15 · shoulder circles 30 sec
  • Empty-bar bench press 2 × 20
Primary · Pectoralis Major
Flat Barbell Bench Press
4 × 6–8Rest 90sProgressive
Retract scapula, slight arch. Touch sternum, drive feet into floor. Add 2.5 kg when you hit 8 clean reps for 2 sessions.
Upper Chest · Clavicular Head
Incline Dumbbell Press
3 × 10–12Rest 75s30° angle
Elbows at 45° to torso — not flared. Squeeze at top, long stretch at bottom. No bouncing.
Chest · Isolation / Stretch
Cable Cross-over / Pec Deck
3 × 15Rest 60sLight–Med
Slow 3-second eccentric. Arms slightly bent, focus on the squeeze not the weight.
Triceps · Lateral Head
Cable Tricep Pushdown (V-bar)
4 × 12Rest 60s
Elbows pinned to ribs throughout. Full extension at bottom, pause 1 sec. Don't lean forward.
Triceps · Long Head
Overhead Dumbbell Extension
3 × 121 DB · 2 handsRest 60s
Long head only fully stretched overhead. Control descent. Core tight to avoid lumbar hyperextension.
Triceps · All 3 Heads
Tricep Dips (Parallel Bars)
3 × FailureTorso uprightRest 75s
Keep torso vertical — leaning forward shifts load to chest. Full lockout at top.

Conventional Deadlift

Bar over mid-foot, lats braced (protect armpits), drive floor away. King of all exercises.
Lats · Width Builder

Full hang at bottom, chin over bar at top. If pull-ups not yet possible, use lat pulldown with same rep scheme.

Torso at 45–60°, brace core. Pull bar to lower chest. Squeeze shoulder blades at top.

3 × 12Rest 75s
No swinging — control the eccentric. Full stretch at front, hard squeeze at back.
Biceps · Long + Short Head
Barbell / EZ-Bar Bicep Curl
4 × 10Elbows fixedRest 60s
Supinate the wrist at the top. No body English. Slow 2-sec eccentric for maximum TUT.
Brachialis · Brachioradialis

3 × 12–15AlternatingRest 60s
Neutral grip throughout — do not supinate. Builds brachialis which pushes bicep peak up visually.

3 × 60s each sideHold each position

2 × 45s eachBoth arms
After heavy pressing, anterior delts get tight. These two stretches actively counter that postural issue.
